# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim for a respectful, technically rigorous collaboration space. We welcome
participants regardless of background, identity, experience, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent while discussing evidence and design.
- Distinguish observed facts, measured results, source-level behavior, and
  hypotheses.
- Respect the project’s clean-room boundary: use public or authorized sources
  and original work only.
- Protect privacy and security: do not post secrets, private captures,
  personal data, or confidential material.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained disruption, doxxing, sharing
private or confidential material, or presenting unsupported claims as proven
results are not acceptable.

## Reporting concerns

For conduct concerns, use the repository’s [Security tab](../../security) when
private reporting is available. Do not include sensitive material in a public
issue. Maintainers may remove content or take other proportionate action to
keep participation safe and constructive.
