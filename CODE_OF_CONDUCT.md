# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want a respectful, technically careful place to work together. Everyone is
welcome, whatever their background, identity, experience, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent when discussing results and design.
- Keep observations, measurements, and guesses clearly apart.
- Follow the clean-room rules: use only public or authorized sources and your
  own work.
- Protect privacy and security: do not post secrets, private captures, personal
  data, or confidential material.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained disruption, doxxing, sharing
private or confidential material, and presenting unsupported claims as proven
are not acceptable.

## Reporting concerns

Report conduct concerns through the repository's [Security tab](../../security)
when private reporting is available, and keep sensitive material out of public
issues. Maintainers may remove content or take other proportionate action to
keep participation safe.
